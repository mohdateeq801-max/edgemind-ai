What the platform does for a live account

Everything is read from the MT5 account itself. Trades the platform cannot attribute stay marked UNKNOWN; nothing is guessed.

Live account

Balance, equity, floating P&L, margin and every open trade with the money at TP, the money at SL and a win-chance estimate.

Strategy intelligence

Each EA and strategy number (EDGE, Claude Analyst, Telegram groups, TradingTop) with win rate, profit factor, drawdown and whether it is working, weakening or failing.

AI market brain

Eleven analysis agents and a direction model tested walk-forward against a baseline. If it has no skill, it says so.

Digital twin

What if a strategy was switched off, risk halved or a trade not closed by hand? Replayed on real candles, kept apart from real results.

Risk and exposure

Open risk if every stop is hit, currency and gold exposure, correlated positions and drawdown alerts.

Reports and alerts

CSV and PDF reports, alerts for missing stop losses, failing strategies and stale data, and Claude-written reviews.
